## Punch list — 12 root causes, ranked

### P1 — wrong numbers shown to the user

**1. Created campaigns and campaign edits never reach 5 of the 6 Advertising grids.**
Tests: PF-21, GF-61. Spans Profile · Portfolio · Placement · ASIN · Ads.
All five seed from `useProfileFilter(ALL_CAMPAIGNS)` (bundle 4468 / 4547 / 4626 / 4677 / 4743;
`Ads.jsx:1897 / 1976 / 2055 / 2106 / 2172`) with no `createdStore.get('campaigns')` and no
`usePersistentOverrides('ads-campaigns')`. Campaigns, Ad Groups, Targeting and Tagging all merge
both. Launch one campaign and `/ads/campaigns` reports 41 while `/ads/profile` still says 40; pause
a campaign and its Ad row still reads "Enabled"; the Daily Budget total is short by the new budget.
The Ads grid's "creatives 1:1 with campaigns" invariant breaks on the first launch.
**Fix:** one shared `useAllCampaigns()` hook returning `[...createdStore.get('campaigns'),
...ALL_CAMPAIGNS]` with the overrides map applied, used by all five.

**2. Tagging compare deltas divide USD by native currency.**
Test: TG-27. `buildRow` USD-normalizes a tag's Spend/Sales when its members span currencies
(bundle 4232-4233) but `prevRowsById` does not convert at all (bundle 4243-4250; `Ads.jsx:1704-1715`).
`DeltaChip` then compares mismatched units. At `FX_USD = {us:1, ca:0.73, uk:1.26}` a CA-weighted tag
shows ~−27pp and a UK-weighted tag ~+26pp of fabricated period change on Spend, Sales and ROAS. The
KPI row directly above converts both sides, so the two disagree on the same tag.
**Fix:** extract the money-normalization out of `buildRow` into a helper and use it for both sides.

**3. Profile CPC / AOV totals are unconverted blends labelled with a sort-dependent symbol.**
Test: PF-09. `foot: (rs) => cur(aggregate(rs).cpc, symOf(rs), 2)` (bundle 4497, 4506;
`Ads.jsx:1926, 1935`). `aggregate()` is FX-free and `symOf(rs)` reads `rs[0].profileId` — so
re-sorting the grid changes the currency symbol on the total without changing the number, and no
"(USD est.)" flag appears. ACoS and ROAS totals share the root cause with smaller magnitude.
**Fix:** add `adsRatioFoot(num, den, dec)` alongside `adsMoneyFoot` and use it for cpc/cpa/aov/acos/
roas. `adNativeMetricCols()` (bundle 4437-4452) carries the identical pattern.

**4. KPI tiles hardcode `$` and contradict the grid beneath them.**
Tests: GF-59, GF-60. `KpiRow` calls `money(a.spend)` with no symbol and `money(n, symbol='$')`
defaults to dollars (bundle 3295-3296, 666; `Ads.jsx:725-726`). On a UK profile the Total row renders
`£98,180` while the tile above renders `$98.2K` for the same number. Separately, Portfolio/Placement/
ASIN rows are pre-converted and carry no `profileId`, so `KpiRow`'s `mixed` check is false and the
"(USD est.)" flag never renders on the tiles — while the Total row on the same page does show it.
**Fix:** pass `sym` and `note` into `KpiRow` (every grid already computes both) instead of
re-deriving them from row shape. Shared component — also fixes Campaigns and Profile.

### P2 — dead affordances that promise something they don't do

**5. Drill-through links carry no scoping param.**
Tests: PF-04, GF-17, GF-43. Profile→Campaigns (`to="/ads/campaigns"`, bundle 4492, title "View
campaigns for this profile"), Portfolio→Campaigns (bundle 4566), ASIN→Product Center
(`to="/commerce/products"`, bundle 4695). The bundle's only URL-param readers are `?tag` (3309),
`?camp` (3519) and `?tab` (3972) — nothing consumes `?profile`, `?portfolio` or `?asin`. Every row's
link lands on the same unfiltered page; the tooltip is the only thing implementing the promise.
**Fix:** emit the param and add the reader, mirroring the working `?camp=` pattern at bundle 4761.

**6. Neither AI grid is selectable — §13's ☑ column, Bulk Operation and Select Current are absent.**
Tests: CA-23, PA-24. `selectable` is passed only at bundle 3481/3601/3718/4371 (the four Ads grids);
Campaign AI (6224) and Product AI (6390) pass none, so DataGrid's checkbox branches never render.
"Bulk Operation" has three bundle hits, all of them the `/ads/bulk` nav label, crumb and head.
There is no way to act on more than one row on either surface.
**Fix:** mirror the Ads pages — `selectable selected onToggle onToggleAll` + a bulk bar
(Pause/Resume AI on Campaign AI, reusing `toggleAI`'s override write).

**7. "Launch AI" split-button dropdown (single / in batches) was never built.**
Tests: CA-18, PA-19. Both are plain `<Btn>` (bundle 6214, 6369; `Automation.jsx:879, 1034`);
`SplitBtn` and `Batches` have 0 occurrences bundle-wide. Campaign AI's modal is nonetheless titled
"Launch AI for a **Single** Tag", advertising a sibling flow that doesn't exist. On Product AI the
receiving side is already correct — `onLaunch(rowset)` takes an array and the toast pluralises — but
`submit` always passes one element, so the plural branch is unreachable dead code.
**Fix:** wrap in the existing `Popover` (as `ExportMenu` does) with two items; batch mode opens the
modal with `<select multiple>`. Product AI is the cheap one — the array signature already lands.

**8. Launch AI resolves to the wrong profile, and the row silently vanishes.**
Tests: CA-22, PA-22. Campaign AI hardcodes `const pid = level === 'tag' ? 'us' : …`
(bundle 6126; `Automation.jsx:791`). Product AI dedupes its catalog with
`new Map(productAI.map(r => [r.asin, r]))` (bundle 6277; `Automation.jsx:942`) — last-wins, so 6 of
8 selectable products resolve to `ca`. Under a mismatched profile the launch toasts success and
persists to `chcreated:`, then the row is filtered out of view; under All Profiles it renders in the
wrong currency. Neither modal has a Profile control.
**Fix:** default to the app's active `profileId` and key the catalog by `asin|profileId`. One
pattern closes both.

### P3 — data-fidelity and UX

**9. Export/filter schemas are strict subsets of the visible columns.**
Tests: PF-20, GF-62, GF-30, GF-42, PA-17. One `*_FIELDS` array feeds both `FilterBar` and
`ExportMenu`, so anything missing from it is neither filterable nor exportable. Missing:
`impr/clk/ctr/cpc/cvr/aov` on all four E5 grids (`CAMPAIGN_FIELDS` has all 18); `bidAdj` on Placement
— the one column that makes that grid distinct; `units`/`asp` on ASIN — likewise; `monthlyCap` and
`campaigns` on Profile — Monthly Cap being the entire reason that page reads `budgets`; `owner` on
Product AI. Worse, Profile exports `$`, `C$` and `£` figures in one Spend column with no currency
column to disambiguate them.
**Fix:** extend each array; or give `ExportMenu` its own `exportFields` so the two schemas can
diverge without dropping columns.

**10. Tagging sub-tag rows break three grid contracts once expanded.**
Tests: TG-12, TG-15, TG-16.
- *Sort scatters children* — page builds parent→child adjacency, `DataGrid.sortedAll` re-sorts the
  flat array with no parent awareness (bundle 1538-1548). The source concedes it in a comment
  (`Ads.jsx:1755`). A sub-tag's numbers end up as a peer of real tags whose members it duplicates.
- *Total row over-counts* — `totalEntries = rows.length` (bundle 1565) counts children while every
  `foot()` strips them (bundle 4089-4096). Expand 2 sub tags on a 6-tag list → `Total · 8` beside
  metrics covering 6.
- *Select-all never engages* — `onToggleAll` populates parents only (bundle 4298) while `allOn` is
  evaluated over `pageRows` including child ids (bundle 1579).
**Fix:** a `rowIsSubtotal` predicate on `DataGrid` covering count, selection and sort anchoring.

**11. Match Tag Rule live preview ignores the global Profile selector.**
Test: TG-23. `TagRuleModal` never references `profileId` (bundle 4176-4206) while the grid scopes via
`baseEnts` (bundle 4216). On Amazon UK (8 of 40 campaigns) the preview reports the all-profile match
count next to a row showing the UK-only count — and the user reads the preview as a validation of
what the tag will contain.
**Fix:** pass the scoped list in, or label the preview "across all profiles".

**12. Campaign AI tab switch clobbers the other tab's saved column layout.**
Test: CA-14. The grid id is dynamic (`campaign-ai-${level}`, bundle 6224) but `DataGrid` reads
`gridStore.get(id)` only in `useState` initializers (`ui.jsx:220-224`), which never re-run without a
remount — and there's no `key`. The persist effect then fires on the id change and writes the
*previous* tab's layout to the new key, destroying what was there.
**Fix:** `key={\`campaign-ai-${level}\`}` on the DataGrid (one line). The `ads-tags-<kind>` grid has
the identical latent bug, so the stronger fix is an id-change effect inside `DataGrid`.

## Explicitly verified as NOT defective

Checked adversarially and cleared — recorded so a later session doesn't re-litigate them:

- **The v0.14.0 Ads-grid double-conversion bug is genuinely fixed** (GF-50/51), and the same defect
  does **not** exist on Portfolio, Placement or ASIN (GF-58). Numerically: correct KPI Spend
  $880,647; a pre-converted variant would under-report at $828,620 (−5.9%).
- **All six grids reconcile exactly** (GF-57): Campaigns · Profile · Portfolio · Placement · ASIN ·
  Ads all compute $880,647.30 → `$880.6K`, sales $2,477,338.20. Exact, not approximate.
- **Placement weights sum to exactly 1.0** for all six metrics, at every date range, with ≤1-unit
  integer rounding drift and zero money drift (GF-21/22).
- **No metric column is missing a `foot:`** on any of the four E5 grids (GF-09/36); no preset
  references an undefined column key (GF-63).
- **All localStorage keys are symmetric and their reads are consumed** — `chgrid:`/`chfilter:`/
  `chplan:` × 4 E5 ids (GF-64), `chedits:campaign-ai-state`, `chcreated:campaign-ai`,
  `chcreated:product-ai` (CA-05/21, PA-23). No `chgoals`-style write-only key on the new surfaces.
- **ROAS colour-coding on Campaign AI is per-row**, comparing `r.roas30` to `r.targetRoas`, not to a
  module constant (CA-06).
- **Managed-event counts scale exactly once** — `scaleRow` passes `ev*` fields through untouched, so
  `ManagedEvents`' `f = days/30` is the sole scaling; no double-scale (PA-03).
- **Sparkline arrays are length-8** in both seeds and launch payloads, and `MiniLine` is
  length-agnostic (PA-09).
- **Enum/mock agreement is exact** for `aiState`, `budgetControl`, `state`, `budgetType`, `strategy`,
  Placement and Ad-Type labels. Only drift: an unused `'Amazon UK'` filter option on both AI
  surfaces (no UK seeds) — cosmetic, filed as a note not a fail.
- **Nav order matches the specified Pacvue Advertising order exactly** (GF-01).
